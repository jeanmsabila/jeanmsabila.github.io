Ph.D. student in Economics
West Virginia University

I study questions in health economics, development economics, and applied microeconomics. My ongoing research examines health policy, household well-being, and the ways public policies shape individual outcomes.

Before my doctoral studies, I worked as an economist in Tanzania’s public service, including as Principal Economist (Minister Counselor) at the Embassy of the United Republic of Tanzania in Washington, D.C.
